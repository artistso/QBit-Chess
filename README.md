# Quantum Wizard Chess 3D (Android)

An Android application built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, featuring:

- **Full Chess Engine**: Standard legal move generation, in-check validation, castling (kingside O-O & queenside O-O-O), checkmate/stalemate detection.
- **Quantum Physics & Coherence**:
  - **Hodge Cage Force Fields**: Interconnected electron lattice shells absorbing kinetic combat strikes with visual flare indicators.
  - **Decoherence Multi-Hit Mode**: Pieces possess a quantum coherence health scalar (1.0 to 0.0), absorbing hits while coherent and shattering when depleted.
  - **EPR Bell-State Quantum Entanglement**: Entangled pieces transmit 38% decoherence to their twin via spooky action at a distance, with catastrophic 55% rupture backlash upon collapse.
  - **Procedural Supersonic Mach Shockwaves & Audio Synthesizer**: Procedural acoustic synthesis producing piece-unique sound signatures (Pawn needle clatter, Knight double thunder, Bishop resonant harmonic beam, Rook seismic rumble, Queen singularity vortex, King monarch cathedral gong).
- **Tactical Chess AI**: Single-player auto-play opponent with material weighting, center control, pawn advancement, and tactical threat prevention. Pass-and-Play 2-player mode is also supported.
- **Interactive Quantum Laboratory**: Real-time HUD and modal drawer with force field toggles, live EPR Bell-state telemetry monitors, and sonic boom test benches.
- **Material 3 Design**: Dark Gothic Sanctum color scheme with gold accents, responsive edge-to-edge layout, and accessibility compliance.
